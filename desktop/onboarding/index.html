Welcome to Onward

Your job application companion. Paste a job posting and Onward reads it, tailors your resume to it, writes a cover letter if you want one, and keeps track of every application until you get an offer.

  • Your files stay yoursResumes, letters and applications are ordinary files in a folder on this computer.
  • Your own AI subscriptionOnward works through Claude Code or Codex, signed in on this computer.
  • You stay in chargeNothing is ever sent or submitted for you.

Setup takes a few minutes: choose a folder, connect your AI tool, then bring in your resume.

Where should Onward save your documents?

Onward keeps everything in one folder: your resumes, every application with its tailored CV and letter, and your progress. We recommend your Documents folder, where it is easy to find and back up.

Setting one up takes about five minutes

  1. Follow How to install above for Claude Code or Codex.
  2. Open Terminal and sign in once with the command below. It opens your browser to log in with your subscription. claude or codex login
  3. Come back here. Onward checks again on its own.

Your folder is ready

One last step, in Onward itself: bring in the resume you have now.

  1. Add your resume. Drop in its PDF, or paste the text. Your AI tool sorts it into sections, word for word.
  2. It becomes two resumes. A master that holds everything you have done, and a general one, ready to send.
  3. Then add a job. Onward tailors a CV from them for each posting, and you send it yourself.

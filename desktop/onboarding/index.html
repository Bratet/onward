Welcome to Onward

Your job application companion. Paste a job posting and Onward reads it, tailors your resume to it, writes a cover letter if you want one, and keeps track of every application until you get an offer.

  • Your files stay yours. Resumes, letters and applications are saved as ordinary files in a folder on this computer.
  • Your own AI subscription. Onward works through Claude Code or Codex, signed in on this computer.
  • You stay in charge. Nothing is sent or submitted for you.

Where should Onward save your documents?

Onward keeps everything in one folder: your resumes, every application with its tailored CV and letter, and your progress. Pick a place you back up.

You're all set

Here's how to get going:

  1. Make the resume yours. Profile opens first: replace the example experience with your own. Every tailored CV is cut from it.
  2. Add a job. Choose New application and paste a posting.
  3. Review, then apply. Check the tailored CV, download it, and send it yourself.
